Crux 1.0.12
- Security: the Instagram, LinkedIn and Hardened Browser screens now enforce their link allowlist, tracker blocking and crash protection, and show the real address
- Fixed a crash when tapping Post in the compose sheet or Share in the carousel studio
- App Lock now covers dialogs and sheets, pauses web pages while locked, and needs your PIN to turn off
- AI agent only opens links you typed or that came from your feeds, and asks before opening links in replies
- Safer link, feed and image handling: blocks tricks that reach your home network, crafted feeds that freeze the app, and in-app links hidden in articles
- Backups can no longer change your AI settings or overwrite your feeds
- Local Ollama is now off unless you set its address yourself
- Release builds are signed in a separate, isolated CI step
